X & Y Grid

5 × 5
12 Xs · 13 Ys · randomly arranged

Choose 5 cells sharing edges. Picks that trap unpicked cells outside groups divisible by 5 are blocked, including dead ends while building a region. Each region takes its majority’s color: green for X and purple for Y, or red and blue in color mode. Ties are gray. Select New region after selecting 5 cells to keep this group and start another. Gold outlines mark your current region. Shuffle resets all regions.
